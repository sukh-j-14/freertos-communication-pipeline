03 / UNDER THE HOOD

Three tasks.
One bounded handoff.

Queue for packets

Eight copied packet values decouple producer and receiver. A full queue causes an explicit drop.

Mutex for metrics

Native tasks share counters under a mutex. A completion semaphore tells the monitor when production ends.

Faults you can explain

Overflow, a stalled receiver, idle timeouts and checksum faults make the tradeoffs visible. No retransmission or RF signal processing is implemented.
